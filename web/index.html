    SpaceCam

    Sky around Earth

    Earth is lit by the real Sun and rotated to sidereal time. Around it is the whole sky from SPHEREx DR1. The grid splits it into 72 sectors A01–F12: click a sector to select it, double-click to fly into the sky.

    Images and wavelengths

    Our own HEALPix/HiPS engine streams tiles straight from NASA/IPAC IRSA and CDS: SPHEREx in 102 infrared channels, AllWISE, 2MASS, Pan-STARRS, DSS2.

    Compare

    One patch of sky over 40 years: DSS2 (1985–2000), 2MASS (1997–2001), AllWISE (2010), Pan-STARRS (2010–2014), SPHEREx (2025–2026) and individual SPHEREx visits. Blink, swipe, difference, animation of every image, and detection of what appeared, vanished or moved.

    Open data

    Every image links to its original in the archive. A GitHub Actions pipeline collects fresh SPHEREx visits for selected fields every week. The code is open (MIT).

    IRSA proxy

    Archive access is configured automatically on the hosted site. You can optionally set your own HTTPS proxy here. Archive availability and image coverage vary.
